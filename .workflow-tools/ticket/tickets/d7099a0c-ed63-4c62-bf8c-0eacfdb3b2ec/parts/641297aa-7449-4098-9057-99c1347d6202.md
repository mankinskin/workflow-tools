Acceptance criteria amendment (agreed with requester 2026-09-02): the original phrasing 'cargo bench ... runs to completion and produces Criterion output for every scenario' is replaced. Full un-timeboxed statistical Criterion runs are not required.

New acceptance criteria:
- Each scenario in the matrix is run individually (not the whole matrix in one invocation) as a real Criterion measurement (NOT --test dry-run mode), filtered to its exact scenario id.
- Each individual scenario invocation is wrapped in its own external timeout of 60s (flat first-pass cap; may be revised to tiered budgets once real baseline numbers exist).
- Each scenario is classified PASS (real Criterion mean/stddev captured within the 60s budget) or EXCEEDED (killed at the 60s cap; record elapsed wall time and any progress Criterion printed before the kill).
- Every scenario in the matrix must have a recorded PASS or EXCEEDED classification -- an EXCEEDED result is acceptable, complete evidence; it is not a failure requiring a rerun.
- The prior --test smoke-mode results (72/75 Success) are NOT valid evidence toward this criterion: --test mode is a correctness-only dry run and collects zero timing data. All 75 scenarios must be (re-)run for real under the above regime.
- Criterion overhead (default warm-up/measurement/sample-size) may be tuned down for this harness (e.g. shorter warm-up/measurement time, smaller sample count) so fast scenarios don't waste their budget on Criterion's own fixed overhead, as long as real per-scenario timing is still produced.
